> **Development** includes learning and maturation of organism. The organism goes through huge _morphological changes_ as it grows

## **Development Robotics and Embodied AI**

**Develop Robotics**
- Strong emphasis to the embodiment of the #agent and on the environment
- Avoids pre-programmed behaviour to solve tasks: rather initiates and maintains 
- Bootstrap of motor and cognitive skills. start with a minimal set of functions and build more functionalities step by step
